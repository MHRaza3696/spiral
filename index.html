About Spiral Group

A Tanzanian business consultancy with 30+ years inside the numbers.

Spiral Group Limited is a Dar es Salaam-based business consultancy and authorized Zoho partner. Led by chartered accountants and Zoho-certified consultants, we have spent careers inside Tanzanian businesses — from fuel-station groups to hospitality brands, manufacturers to consultancies.

We stay small on purpose. The partner you meet on day one is the partner who shows up on the long term. Our work blends classical accounting practice with modern cloud finance systems, and the result is operational clarity that scales without breaking.
