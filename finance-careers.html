Initiative

Empowering the next generation of finance professionals

We're building a bridge between talented finance professionals and the businesses that need them — powered by Zoho certification and practical skills training.

The vision

Tanzania's growing economy needs skilled finance professionals who can operate modern cloud-based accounting systems. Yet most graduates enter the workforce with theoretical knowledge but no practical experience with tools like Zoho Books, Zoho CRM, or Zoho Analytics.

Spiral Group, in partnership with EPLS, is launching a comprehensive programme to close this gap — combining Zoho certification training with a recruitment platform that connects trained professionals with businesses seeking their expertise.

What we're building

✓

Zoho certification training

Structured training programme delivered by EPLS-certified instructors covering Zoho Books, CRM, and the broader Zoho ecosystem.

✓

CV database & candidate profiles

A searchable database of trained and certified candidates, complete with skills profiles, certifications, and availability status.

✓

Job board for finance roles

Listed opportunities from businesses across Tanzania seeking Zoho-skilled finance professionals — from bookkeepers to financial controllers.

✓

Matching & placement

Active matching of candidates to roles based on skills, experience level, location, and business requirements.
